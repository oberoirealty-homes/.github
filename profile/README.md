1. Search the project or tower on the official Haryana RERA website.
2. Match the registration details with the documents provided by the developer.
3. Review the approved plans and the declared project timeline.
4. Check whether the details on the marketing page match the official records.
5. Read the booking terms and agreement carefully before paying.

If any information is unclear or inconsistent, ask the developer for written clarification and consider getting independent professional advice.

## Understand the Full Cost Before Booking

The advertised price is not always the final amount a buyer will pay. Additional expenses can affect the overall budget, so it is important to request a detailed cost sheet before making a decision.

Ask about the base price, applicable taxes, stamp duty, registration expenses, parking charges, maintenance deposits and any other fees that may apply. You should also understand the payment schedule and the terms associated with each payment milestone.

If you plan to take a home loan, consider the monthly repayment alongside your other financial commitments. A property should fit your long-term budget, not just your initial purchasing capacity.

Keep copies of the cost sheet, payment terms and other written commitments so you can refer to them later.
